Fairness & audit

Review how the screening process behaves across groups. Look for questions, not quick conclusions.

ⓘ

Fairness is an ongoing review. Group comparisons are descriptive signals. Small samples, missing data and different student needs can affect the results. Never use group membership to calculate an individual student's risk.
